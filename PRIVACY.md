# Athena Music – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

Athena Music plays music from **your own** Navidrome (Subsonic-compatible) server. It has no accounts of its own, ads, analytics or crash reporting, and the developer receives no data from it.

## What the app sends
The app talks only to the server address you enter. It sends your username and credentials to that server and requests your library and audio streams from it. The server is run by you or whoever you chose, and what it does with the data is up to them.

## What is stored on your device
The server address and username, your password (in Android's encrypted storage) and a cached copy of your artist list, so it appears when you are offline. "Sign out" in Settings removes the address, username, password and cache. Uninstalling removes everything.

## Permissions
- Internet and local-network access: to reach your server.
- Notifications and a foreground service: to show playback controls and keep music playing with the screen off.

## Sharing
Nothing is shared with the developer or any third party. The app reads theme, typeface and text size from Athena Launcher if it is installed on the same phone.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects nothing about anyone.

## Open source
The full source code is public, so every claim here can be checked: https://github.com/Larmen/athena-music

## Contact and changes
Questions or requests: open an issue at https://github.com/Larmen/athena-music/issues. If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address with a new date.
